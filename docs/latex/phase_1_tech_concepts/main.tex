\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
The Agentic Market Panic Simulator (AMPS) addresses a critical gap in economic modeling: simulating the irrational, sentiment-driven dynamics of macroeconomic contagion at a microsecond scale. Utilizing a swarm of 400 generative Large Language Models (LLMs) interacting with a deterministic Limit Order Book (LOB), AMPS bridges behavioral finance and bare-metal high-performance computing. This book presents a cohesive architectural narrative detailing the empirical necessity of traditional finance datasets, the mitigation of computational bottlenecks via Singleflight request coalescing and zero-copy Apache Arrow data pipelines, and the hardware-level necessity of relying on SSE4.2 \texttt{popcnt} intrinsics over AVX-512 in heterogeneous CPU topologies. Through Matryoshka Representation Learning and 1.58-bit ternary quantization, AMPS achieves latency-optimized generative simulation on consumer-grade hardware.
